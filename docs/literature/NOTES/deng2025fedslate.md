# deng2025fedslate: Deng et al., FedSlate (IEEE TETCI 2025)

- **Status:** IEEE Trans. Emerging Topics in Computational Intelligence 9(6):4202-4216 (2025), doi 10.1109/TETCI.2025.3573250; arXiv:2409.14872.
- **Evidence level:** E2 (introduction and experimental setup skimmed).
- **Claim (own words):**
  - A federated extension of SlateQ for cross-platform recommendation.
  - Evaluated in a RecSim-built environment against SlateQ and random baselines.
- **Overlap:** none with our RQs. There is no myopic baseline, and catalog size, slate size and choice model are not varied as research variables, as far as read.
- **Use:** evidence that SlateQ and RecSim are still used as a base in 2024-2025 work.
