# chen2019topk: Chen et al. 2019 (WSDM 2019)

- **Status:** WSDM 2019, pp. 456-464, doi 10.1145/3289600.3290999. Peer-reviewed.
- **Evidence level:** E2 (abstract and introduction).
- **Claim (own words):**
  - Scales REINFORCE to a production recommender with millions of actions.
  - Corrects for logged-data bias with off-policy correction, including a top-K correction for recommending several items at once.
  - Evaluated in simulation and in live YouTube experiments.
- **Use:** context. It is a policy-gradient alternative to value decomposition for multi-item recommendation. No overlap with our RQs.
