## Abstract content (own words)

- Frames recommendation as a slate-MDP and compares FullSlateQ with SlateQ under several simulated preference scenarios.
- Reports SlateQ about 10.57 % better than FullSlateQ in non-static environments.
- Reports that both agents behave short-term with a moderate discount factor.
