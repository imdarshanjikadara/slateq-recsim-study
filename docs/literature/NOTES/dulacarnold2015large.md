# dulacarnold2015large: Dulac-Arnold et al. 2015 (arXiv preprint)

- **Status:** arXiv:1512.07679, preprint.
- **Evidence level:** E2 (abstract and introduction).
- **Claim (own words):**
  - For very large discrete action sets, embed actions in a continuous space, let an actor output a proto-action, and retrieve nearby discrete actions by approximate nearest neighbours (the "Wolpertinger" architecture).
  - Recommender systems are named as a motivating case.
- **Use:**
  - Context for scaling RL to large catalogs.
  - Singha Roy 2024 combines this idea with SlateQ (see singharoy2024news).
