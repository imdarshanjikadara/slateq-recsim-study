# sunehag2015slatemdp: Sunehag et al. 2015 (arXiv preprint)

- **Status:** arXiv:1512.01124, preprint.
- **Evidence level:** E2 (abstract and introduction).
- **Claim (own words):**
  - Introduces slate-MDPs: the agent picks a slate of primitive actions, of which at most one is executed, and the executed action may not even come from the slate.
  - Learns whole-slate values with deep Q-learning on state and action features.
  - Uses attention and sequential greedy selection to keep the slate choice tractable.
- **Use:** foundation of the slate-MDP formulation that SlateQ builds on. No overlap with our RQs.
